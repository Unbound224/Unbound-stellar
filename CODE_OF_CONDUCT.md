# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is adapted from it; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Unbound community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who will rely on this software

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Misrepresenting the project's capabilities to users (for example claiming Mainnet availability, partnerships or
  guaranteed liquidity that do not exist)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when an individual
is officially representing the project. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the
maintainers of the [Unbound224](https://github.com/Unbound224) organization (for example through a private
vulnerability report if no other private channel is available). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
privacy of the reporter respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
